Questions from testers

What do I need to do as a tester?

Just study the way you normally would. In the app, go to You, then Get help or send feedback, and turn on I'm a beta tester: on some days a short question shows on Today, and answering is optional. If something is confusing, broken, or an answer looks wrong, tap ··· on the answer screen and choose Report a problem with this question, or reply to our email.

Will I keep my progress?

Yes. KaiSip updates on your phone like any app, and your progress stays.

How are the questions made?

Drafted with AI tools under the direction of a CISA-certified founder, then checked by automated and AI review checks. The founder reads the reports and decides the fixes. AI can make mistakes, so tell us anything that looks wrong. How questions are made

What happens to my details?

We used your answers on the form to add you to the Google Play test, and we use them to email you about the beta, credit you if you asked, give you the tester reward, and learn which certifications to add next. The maintainer also keeps a private record of your test dates and feedback to check the reward. We never sell your details. The Privacy Policy (section 3e) has the details.

How do I leave the test?

Leave the test in Google Play, or email certprep.support@gmail.com and we'll take you off the list. If you leave before you qualify, we can't give you the tester reward.
