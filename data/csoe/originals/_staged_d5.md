## PCAOB

- PCAOB AS 2601.02 (Definitions: user organization, user auditor, service organization and service auditor)
  - Verified (owner-supplied copy of the pcaobus.org page, saved 4 Nov 2026): https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2601; checked .02: the user organization engaged the service organization and has its financial statements audited; the user auditor reports on those statements; the service organization provides services that are part of the user organization's information system; the service auditor reports on the service organization's controls that may be relevant to a user organization's internal control as it relates to a financial statement audit; .02 also defines the two report types restated in .24.
- PCAOB AS 2601.03 (Services that are part of the information system)
  - Verified (owner-supplied copy of the pcaobus.org page, saved 4 Nov 2026): https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2601; checked .03: a service organization's services are part of an entity's information system if they affect any of its significant classes of transactions, the automated and manual procedures by which transactions are initiated, recorded, processed and reported into the financial statements, the related accounting records and accounts, how the system captures other significant events and conditions, or the financial reporting process, estimates and disclosures included; examples are bank trust departments, mortgage servicers and application service providers; the section is not meant for services limited to executing transactions the client specifically authorized (a bank processing checking account transactions, a broker executing securities trades) or for financial interests in partnerships, corporations and joint ventures reported to interest holders.
- PCAOB AS 2601.21 (No reference to the service auditor's report and no division of responsibility)
  - Verified (owner-supplied copy of the pcaobus.org page, saved 4 Nov 2026): https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2601; checked .14 and .21: the user auditor remains responsible for evaluating the service auditor's evidence and its effect on the control risk assessment (.14); the user auditor should not refer to the service auditor's report as a basis, in part, for the opinion on the user organization's financial statements, because the service auditor examines no portion of those statements, so responsibility for the audit cannot be divided (.21).
- PCAOB AS 2601.22 (Responsibilities of service auditors)
  - Verified (owner-supplied copy of the pcaobus.org page, saved 4 Nov 2026): https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2601; checked .22: the service auditor is responsible for the representations in its own report and for due care in the procedures behind them; the engagement should follow the relevant PCAOB auditing standards; the service auditor should be independent of the service organization but need not be independent of each user organization.
- PCAOB AS 2601.24 (Two types of service auditor's report)
  - Verified (owner-supplied copy of the pcaobus.org page, saved 4 Nov 2026): https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2601; checked .02, .12 and .24: the service organization establishes the type of engagement and report, ideally after discussion with user organizations; a report on controls placed in operation covers the description, suitability of design and placement in operation as of a specific date and is not meant to support reducing assessed control risk (.24a; .12: not intended to give any evidence of operating effectiveness); a report on controls placed in operation and tests of operating effectiveness adds an opinion on whether the tested controls operated effectively during the period specified and may support such a reduction (.24b). These match the SEC staff FAQ's footnote 3 description of a Type 2 report and the bank's "SOC 1 Type 1" / "Type 2" mapping.
- PCAOB AS 2601.31 (Complementary user organization controls)
  - Verified (owner-supplied copy of the pcaobus.org page, saved 4 Nov 2026): https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2601; checked .31 and .46: when the service organization's system was designed assuming certain controls would be implemented by user organizations, the service auditor should delineate those complementary user organization controls in the description of controls; if users' application of them is necessary to achieve the stated control objectives, the scope and opinion paragraphs are modified to say the objectives are achieved if users applied the controls contemplated in the design; .46 repeats this for reports with tests and adds that dependence on user controls is delineated in the description of tests.
- PCAOB AS 2601.33 (Description of controls remains the service organization's responsibility)
  - Verified (owner-supplied copy of the pcaobus.org page, saved 4 Nov 2026): https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2601; checked .33 and .48: the service organization may prepare the description of controls and control objectives; if the service auditor prepares it, the representations in the description remain the service organization's responsibility (.48 repeats this for reports with tests of operating effectiveness).
- PCAOB AS 2601.44 (Contents of a report with tests of operating effectiveness)
  - Verified (owner-supplied copy of the pcaobus.org page, saved 4 Nov 2026): https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2601; checked .44: among its required contents, the report states the period covered by the tests (g), gives an opinion on whether the tested controls operated with sufficient effectiveness during that period (h), states that the effect of the service organization's controls depends on their interaction with controls and other factors at individual user organizations (j), and states that the service auditor performed no procedures to evaluate the effectiveness of controls at individual user organizations (k).
