- 15 U.S.C. 7215(c)(6) (Failure to supervise)

## 17 CFR (Securities and Exchange Commission)
- 17 CFR 210.2-01(c)(5) (Contingent fees)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/210.2-01; checked an accountant is not independent if, at any point during the audit and professional engagement period, it provides any service or product to an audit client for a contingent fee or a commission, or receives a contingent fee or commission from an audit client; eCFR version history (https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/versions/title-17.json?section=210.2-01) shows no amendment after 9 Jun 2021
- 17 CFR 210.2-01(c)(8) (Compensation)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/210.2-01; checked an accountant is not independent if, at any point during the audit and professional engagement period, any audit partner earns or receives compensation based on procuring engagements with that audit client for products or services other than audit, review or attest services; firms with fewer than ten partners and fewer than five issuer audit clients are exempt; same text in the 2025 CFR edition (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title17-vol3/xml/CFR-2025-title17-vol3-sec210-2-01.xml)
- 17 CFR 210.2-01(f)(3)(ii) (Definition of financial reporting oversight role)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/210.2-01; checked a role in which a person is in a position to or does exercise influence over the contents of the financial statements or anyone who prepares them, such as a board member, chief executive officer, president, chief financial officer, chief operating officer, general counsel, chief accounting officer, controller, director of internal audit, director of financial reporting, treasurer, or any equivalent position
- 17 CFR 210.2-01(f)(7)(ii) (Definition of audit partner)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/210.2-01; checked an audit partner is a partner (or equivalent) on the audit engagement team with responsibility for decision-making on significant auditing, accounting and reporting matters, or who maintains regular contact with management and the audit committee, other than a partner who consults with others on the team regarding technical or industry-specific issues, transactions or events; includes (A) the lead partner, (B) the Engagement Quality Reviewer, (C) other team partners with more than ten hours on the consolidated statements and (D) lead partners on subsidiaries with 20% or more of consolidated assets or revenues
- 17 CFR 210.2-01(f)(10) (Definition of contingent fee)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/210.2-01; checked a contingent fee is any fee under an arrangement in which no fee is charged unless a specified finding or result is attained, or the fee otherwise depends on the finding or result; fees fixed by courts or public authorities, or in tax matters set by judicial proceedings or agency findings, are not contingent; fees may vary depending, for example, on the complexity of services

## SEC releases
- SEC Release No. 33-8183 Section II.A (Conflicts of interest resulting from employment relationships)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/33-8183.htm; checked the SEC's 2003 rules under Section 208(a) apply the Section 206 cooling-off period to any financial reporting oversight role (a fuller test than the four named officers, since the Act also covers equivalent positions); team members other than the lead and concurring partners must have provided more than ten hours to have participated; audit procedures commence the day after the prior annual report is filed; merger and emergency exceptions need audit committee awareness or determination
- SEC Release No. 33-8183 Section II.C.2 (Additional partner rotation)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/33-8183.htm; checked rotation extends to "audit partners" beyond the lead and concurring partners; specialty partners and national office partners consulted on technical accounting or auditing issues are not audit partners and need not rotate; partners on subsidiaries below the 20% threshold are not audit partners
- SEC Release No. 33-8183 Section II.E (Compensation)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/33-8183.htm; checked an accountant is not independent if an audit partner earns compensation based on selling engagements to the audit client for services other than audit, review or attest services; the rule applies to audit partners and exempts firms with fewer than five issuer audit clients and fewer than ten partners
- SEC Release No. 33-8183 Section II.G.3 (Other material written communications)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/33-8183.htm; checked Rule 2-07(a)(3) implements the Act's examples (management letter, schedule of unadjusted differences) and the SEC expects further material communications to include the management representation letter, reports on observations and recommendations on internal controls, the engagement letter and the independence letter; the list is not exhaustive
