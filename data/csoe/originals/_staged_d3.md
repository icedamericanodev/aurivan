## 17 CFR (Securities and Exchange Commission)
- 17 CFR 210.1-02(a)(4) (Definitions of terms related to internal control over financial reporting)
  - Verified (opened 31 Oct 2026): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/210.1-02; checked Rule 1-02(a)(4) of Regulation S-X: material weakness means a deficiency, or a combination of deficiencies, in ICFR (as defined in Rule 13a-15(f) or 15d-15(f)) such that there is a reasonable possibility that a material misstatement of the registrant's annual or interim financial statements will not be prevented or detected on a timely basis; significant deficiency means a deficiency, or combination, less severe than a material weakness yet important enough to merit attention by those responsible for oversight of financial reporting. Same wording as the Rule 12b-2 definitions (https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/240.12b-2, opened 31 Oct 2026). SEC Release No. 33-8810 footnote 46 (https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/interp/2007/33-8810.pdf, opened 31 Oct 2026) names both rules as where the term is defined.
