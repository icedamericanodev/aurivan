# KaiSip marketing plan: standing owner requests

This file is the first place to read whenever marketing comes up: the strategy, the launch plan, the store listing, the promo or marketing video, and social posts. It lists what the owner has asked to be included. The store listing details live in `docs/mobile/STORE_LISTING.md`.

## Must include (owner requests)

### 1. The plant "Turning" (owner, 29 Oct 2026)
The owner wants the plant's form-change animation ("The Turning") in the **revised marketing video and materials**:
- the store preview video;
- the website hero;
- social clips;
- the store screenshots, where a still can carry it.

The owner's words: "I want to include the glimpse of every plant, or if not, highlight it. I think it would amaze prospective users once they watch it."

**How to include it:**
- **A montage.** Show a quick glimpse of each of the 13 species turning (about 1 s each), or a curated highlight of 3–4 of the most striking finals (e.g. narra's Gold Rain, bamboo's Moon Gate, a flowering tree, the oak's Broadcrown). End on one full, slow Form III → IV Turning.
- **Lead with the learning.** The plant grows only from real study, so the clip should show the learning fact before the reveal ("You recalled 30 questions after a week away"). The message is that progress is earned, not bought.
- **Sources.** The motion previews are in the scratchpad (`evolve/motion/out/*.mp4`). Re-record the shipped app (light and dark) with `SHOTS=forms npm run shots` in `mobile/`, plus a screen recording of the reveal.

**Rules:**
- Use original art only.
- Never use the words "Pokémon" or "evolve" in any copy. Say "grows into a new form" / "The Turning".
- No pass claims, and never show the total question count.
- Respect the store rule that a preview video shows real app footage.
