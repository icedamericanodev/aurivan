Three points that guard against stale wording:

1. **"IPPF" is still current.** The Standards and the Topical Requirements
   page describe the 2024 International Professional Practices Framework as
   the Global Internal Audit Standards and the Topical Requirements (both
   mandatory) plus Global Guidance (recommended). Only the 2017 contents of
   the IPPF are stale.
2. **Advisory services are also known as consulting services.** The glossary
   entry for advisory services says so (S9 Glossary). KaiSip uses "advisory
   services" and may teach the older name once.
3. **Domain II replaces the former Code of Ethics.** The opening of Domain II
   says so (S9, Domain II introduction).

### Internal Audit Practitioner route (S13, S8 Appendix D, S10)

The IAP exam is CIA Part 1: 125 multiple-choice questions, 150 minutes, two
years to pass once approved, a government photo ID as the only entry
requirement. An active IAP gives entry to the CIA program without a degree and
a waiver for Part 1, so the holder sits Parts 2 and 3. A CIA candidate who has
passed Part 1 can apply for the IAP. From 2026 IAP holders need 20 hours of
continuing education a year and annual renewal.
