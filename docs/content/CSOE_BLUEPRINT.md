# CSOE blueprint (Certified Sarbanes-Oxley Expert)

Checked 31 Oct 2026 against the issuer's own pages, opened in full: the CSOE
program page (P1), the exam steps PDF (P2) and the FAQ PDF (P3). The owner
also supplied a copy of P2; it is byte-for-byte the file on the issuer's site.
This is the outline KaiSip's CSOE notes and questions follow. Codes 1A1, 1A2 …
are KaiSip's (CRISC style); use them as the `subtopic` prefix (for example
`"3A2 Officer certifications. Penalty tier"`) and as the notes `topic_id`.

The issuer is the Sarbanes Oxley Compliance Professionals Association
(SOXCPA), a business unit of Compliance LLC (P3). Course part titles below are
SOXCPA's (P1); everything else is KaiSip's wording. Sources and their status
are in `docs/content/CSOE_RESEARCH_LOG.md`.

## SOXCPA's course synopsis (P1, summarized)

| Part | SOXCPA's headings |
|---|---|
| 1 | The need; the Sarbanes-Oxley Act; companies affected and whether each provision applies to every company; foreign private issuers; the registration process; EDGAR; case studies; American Depository Receipt (ADR) Levels 1–3; employees affected. Then the Act's key sections, title by title: Title I (Sections 101–109), Title II (201–209), Title III (301–308), Title IV (401–409, plus "what the critics say"), Titles V and VII named, Title VIII (801–807), whistleblowers (after SOX, foreign, after Dodd-Frank), Title IX (901–906), "Sections 302 – 404 – 906", committees and teams |
| 2 | The frameworks: COSO Internal Control—Integrated Framework (1992: the cube, five components, three objectives) and its 2013 update (updated cube, a cyber example); COSO ERM (2004: components, cube, differences from COSO, "is COSO ERM needed for compliance?") and the 2017 update (strategy selection) |
| 3 | The SEC and the Act (the Securities Act of 1933, the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, SEC rulemaking, SEC investigations, common violations, disclosing an investigation, document retention, settlements); the PCAOB and the Act (PCAOB rulemaking; the auditing standards "as amended, effective for audits of financial statements for fiscal years ending on or after December 15, 2020") |
| 4 | Scope of Sarbanes-Oxley (software, spreadsheets and their controls, SAS 70 and its replacement by SSAE 16 and SSAE 18); E-SOX (the EU 8th Company Law Directive: Ahold and Parmalat, Articles 45 and 46); J-SOX (Japan's Financial Instruments and Exchange Law) |
| 5 | The Dodd-Frank Act and the Sarbanes-Oxley amendments: five key objectives, the PCAOB under Dodd-Frank, Basel II/III, the Financial Stability Oversight Council, the Orderly Liquidation Authority, the Federal Insurance Office, the Volcker Rule, the new whistleblower rules, the Sarbanes-Oxley amendment |

SOXCPA says it covers only the parts of the Act relevant to risk management
and compliance (P1). KaiSip follows that scope.

**Currency notes on the course.** Part 2 teaches the 1992 COSO framework and
the 2004 ERM framework alongside their updates; KaiSip teaches the 2013 and
2017 editions as current and the earlier ones as history (where they share
an idea, such as the five components, both agree). Part 3 names the PCAOB
standards as amended for fiscal years ending on or after 15 Dec 2020; KaiSip
checks every standard against the current PCAOB text before keying it (see
"Open items").

## The CSOE lens

Full standard: `.claude/agents/csoe-standards-auditor.md`. In short: answers
follow the letter of the Act and the SEC and PCAOB rules (which section, of
whom, what, by when, with what consequence), not best practice; management
owns and assesses ICFR, the CEO and CFO certify (302 and 906 differ), the
auditor attests (404(b)), the audit committee oversees the auditor, the PCAOB
oversees audit firms, the SEC oversees issuers and the PCAOB. Every threshold
keyed on is checked at its current primary source. SOXCPA's own exam advice
(P2) matches the KaiSip tip voice: cross out the options you know are wrong,
then pick the best of the rest.

## Open items

1. **PCAOB standards.** pcaobus.org answers automated requests with a
   Cloudflare challenge (not a network-policy block), so AS 2201 and the other
   standards cannot be opened. Until then, PCAOB items key on the Act
   (Title I) and SEC rules only, never on an AS paragraph. If the owner
   downloads the current AS 2201 (and AS 1305) PDFs and attaches them, they
   can be checked and cited.
2. **E-SOX and J-SOX (1B).** Need eur-lex.europa.eu without its bot challenge
   (or another official EU host such as op.europa.eu or finance.ec.europa.eu)
   and www.fsa.go.jp or www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp.
3. **Trademark re-check** before store submission (the unrelated "CSOE"
   application may register).
4. **Pending SEC proposals to watch** (checked 31 Oct 2026; not adopted):
   Release No. 33-11419 (File S7-2026-18, May 2026) would remove the
   accelerated filer and smaller reporting company categories and raise the
   large accelerated filer threshold to $2 billion; Release No. 33-11414
   would allow an optional semiannual Form 10-S. If either is adopted, the
   filer-category items and notes (1A2, 1A4, 3B2, 4C3 and their 404(b)
   items) must be re-keyed. `csoe-rules-currency-checker` checks the SEC's
   rulemaking list on every batch.
5. **Not seen:** the official presentations themselves (paid). The exam asks
   only what they answer, so a few KaiSip items may test public law the
   presentations skip, or miss a detail they stress. Owner decision whether
   to enrol (CSOE_BANK_PLAN.md section 8).
6. **Domain experts** for each domain: `.claude/agents/csoe-d{1..5}-domain-expert.md`.
