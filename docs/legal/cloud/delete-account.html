Delete your KaiSip account

Draft prepared: 27 October 2026. Not in effect.

You can delete your KaiSip account and everything it holds at any time: in the app, on the website, or by email. You don’t need to reinstall the app. KaiSip · by [Developer name, exactly as shown on Google Play].

In the app (Android)

  1. Open Settings → Account → Delete account.
  2. Sign in again when asked (with Google or an email code), so we know it’s you.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

On the website

  1. Go to kaisip.com and open Your data → Account → Delete account.
  2. Sign in again when asked, then type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion is immediate, in the app and on the website.

Without the app or the website

Email certprep.support@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my KaiSip account". We reply within 7 days and delete within 30 days.

What we delete

Your email address, account ID, sign-in records and sessions (with the IP addresses and browsers they came from), the name and profile picture link Google shared (if you used Google), the terms you accepted, and your synced progress and settings, including the support copy.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Our encrypted backups are kept for 30 days, so your data is gone from them within 30 days.
  • A record that an account was deleted: a one-way keyed code made from the account ID, the day (not the time) of the deletion, and how many feedback board posts and comments the account had (none during the beta). It holds no email address, and is deleted after 3 years.
  • Our database provider’s request and sign-in event logs (your IP address, browser or app, and the times you signed in) for [Supabase’s log retention period on our plan].
  • Support emails you sent us are kept for up to 12 months after they are resolved.

Deleting your account does not remove the progress kept on your phone or in your browser. To remove that too, reset your progress in the app’s Settings (or delete the app), and clear the website’s data in your browser (or choose "remove my progress from this browser" when you sign out).

Delete only your synced progress

To keep your account but remove the progress it stores, use Settings → Account → Delete synced progress in the app, or Your data → Account on the website, or email us with the subject "Delete my synced progress".
