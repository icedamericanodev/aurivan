Delete your Aurivan account

Aurivan · by [Developer name, exactly as shown on Google Play and the App Store]

You can delete your Aurivan account and its data at any time, in the app or by email. You don’t need to reinstall the app.

In the app

  1. Open Settings → Account → Delete account.
  2. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first, so we know it’s you.

Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

Email certprep.support@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Aurivan account". We confirm within 7 days and delete within 30 days.

What we delete

Your email address, account ID, display name, synced progress (answers, review schedule, saved questions, mock results, tree companion, settings), and your board posts, comments and votes.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Backups clear within 30 days.
  • A record that an account was deleted, made with a one-way code from the account ID. It holds no email address.
  • If we removed a post of yours for breaking the rules, a short record of what was removed and why is kept for up to 12 months.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 12 months after they are resolved.

Deleting your account does not remove progress saved on your phone. To remove that too, reset your progress in Settings or delete the app.

Delete only your synced progress

To keep your account but remove the progress it stores, use Settings → Account → Delete synced progress, or email us with the subject "Delete my synced progress".
