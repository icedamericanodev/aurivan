Community guidelines

Version 2026-11. Draft prepared: 12 October 2026. Not in effect.

The feedback board is where learners help shape KaiSip: ideas, app problems and questions, and what we should fix or build next. Keep it useful and kind.

1. Be kind

No harassment, hate, threats, personal attacks or sexual content. Zero tolerance. Disagree with ideas, never with people.

2. No real exam questions. Ever.

Don’t post, paraphrase, describe or ask for questions from a real certification exam, and don’t offer or ask for “dumps”. Candidates agree to keep exam questions confidential, and sharing them can cost someone their certification. Talk about topics (“I’d like more practice on business continuity test types”), never items.

3. Don’t paste KaiSip’s practice questions or answers either

It spoils them for everyone. If something in one of our questions looks wrong, use Report a problem under the question: it reaches us privately and faster. The board can’t accept text copied from our questions.

4. No copied material

Don’t paste pages from review manuals or other copyrighted material. Describe it in your own words.

5. Keep personal details private

Yours and other people’s: no email addresses, phone numbers, addresses or messaging handles.

6. No ads or spam

No advertising, no link shorteners and no links to exam dumps or paid “real question” sites.

7. Stay on topic, and be yourself

Studying and the app. Don’t pretend to be the KaiSip team, a certification body or anyone else; names that do are refused.

What we do

  • We check some posts before they appear (word lists, and a comparison with our own practice questions). Posts from new members, and posts that look like they might hold real exam content, wait for a person first.
  • We review reports within 24 hours. You can report any post, comment or member from its ⋯ menu (“Report post”, “Report comment”, “Report” and their name). A report of real exam content from an established member hides the post for everyone straight away while we check it.
  • You can block anyone. You won’t see their posts or comments, and they can’t comment on yours. They aren’t told.
  • We remove posts that break these guidelines, and we limit, suspend or close the board access of people who break them seriously or repeatedly. When we remove something of yours, the app tells you why, and you can ask us to look again within 14 days.
  • If we can’t watch the board for a while, we pause posting. Reading and voting keep working.

How we decide what’s next

A person reads every report, and we aim to read every post. Correctness comes first: a wrong answer key or two right answers jumps the queue, whatever the vote count. Then we weigh how many learners it affects (votes, reports, and how much of the exam it covers) and how serious it is. Votes matter, but they don’t decide alone: a small fix that helps everyone can beat a big idea that helps a few. When we decide not to do something, we say why. Shipped work is listed in What’s new, with thanks to the learners who asked for it.

We never copy learners’ posts into the question bank or the study notes.

Takedowns and contact

Questions or concerns: certprep.support@gmail.com. Rights holders: email us with the post’s link. We act within 24 hours.
