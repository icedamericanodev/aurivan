Privacy Policy

Draft prepared: 27 October 2026. Not in effect.

KaiSip is a CISA exam-preparation app, free during the beta and built privacy-first. You don’t need an account. Without one, your study progress never leaves your device. If you choose to create one, we keep only what is needed to sign you in and to sync your progress between the app and the website (section 6A). On the website we use anonymous analytics only if you accept it; the mobile app has no analytics at all. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the KaiSip web application, the KaiSip mobile app for iPhone and Android, and their related pages ("the App"). Sections 2 to 5 describe the website; section 6 describes the mobile app, and section 6A the optional KaiSip account, which works on both. KaiSip is operated as an independent project by its maintainer, [Developer name, exactly as shown on Google Play], in the Philippines ("we", "us"). It is not a company offering, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ISACA.

2. Our privacy approach

The website runs entirely in your browser. Unless you sign in, it talks to no server of ours: every question you answer, your scores, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your own device and are never transmitted to us or anyone else. Signing in is optional. If you do, your progress is synced with your KaiSip account, as section 6A describes.

3. Information involved

3a. On-device data (never transmitted)

Your study progress, scores, bookmarks, review queue, exam-date setting and theme choice are saved in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data for this site removes it.

If you add a first name or nickname for the greeting on Today, it is optional and kept in this browser too. It goes into your progress file only when you export that file yourself, and it is never synced.

If you sign in, this browser also keeps your sign-in session in its storage, so you stay signed in (or only until you close the tab, if you tick "This is a shared computer"). Your progress is then synced with your account (section 6A).

3b. Analytics (only with your consent)

The App uses Google Analytics, configured with Google Consent Mode. Before you make a choice, and if you choose "Decline", analytics storage is disabled — no analytics cookie and no analytics identifier are stored on your device.

Only if you select "Accept" on the cookie banner does Google Analytics record anonymous, aggregated usage (such as page views and which sections are visited) and set a cookie on your device to distinguish visits. This helps us understand how the App is used and where to improve it.

We do not collect names, email addresses, your answers, or your scores through analytics, and we do not use analytics for advertising.

3c. Feedback you choose to send

The App includes optional Feedback and Flag features. If you use one, the message you write is submitted through a Google Form to the maintainer's email inbox. This happens only when you choose to send it. Please do not include sensitive personal information in these messages.

We keep these messages for up to 12 months after your issue is resolved, then delete them, or sooner if you ask.

3d. Hosting logs

The website is hosted on Cloudflare. As with any website, the hosting provider may automatically process technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and protect against abuse. This is handled by the hosting provider under its own terms.

3e. Beta tester sign-up (optional)

If you sign up to test KaiSip before launch, you fill in a Google Form run by the maintainer, outside the App. It collects your email address, your phone type, your country, your exam timing (optional), which other certifications interest you (optional), how you found us, and the name or handle you would like credited (or none). We use it to add you to the Google Play test, to email you about the beta, to credit you if you asked, to give you the tester reward, and to learn which certifications to add next.

It is stored in KaiSip's Google account (Google Forms and Sheets), and Android testers' email addresses are also added to Google Play Console's tester list. If you ask to be credited, the name you chose is shown to everyone in the mobile app's "Thank you, beta testers" screen; ask us at any time and it is removed in the next app update.

We keep sign-up data until your reward has been given and has ended, or until we confirm there will be no paid features, then delete it within 3 months. To leave or ask anything, email certprep.support@gmail.com.

3f. Signing in on the website (optional)

The sign-in form appears only on kaisip.com. If you sign in with Google, Google’s own page asks you to confirm, and Google tells our sign-in system your email address, name and profile picture link (section 6A). If you ask for an email code, a Cloudflare Turnstile check runs on the form first, to stop automated abuse of our email sending; Cloudflare processes technical information about your browser for that check under its own terms and does not use it for advertising.

4. Cookies and consent

The App itself sets no cookies, signed in or not (a sign-in session is kept in browser storage, not in a cookie). The only cookie scenario is Google Analytics, described in section 3b, and it is used only after you accept on the cookie banner. Until you make a choice, analytics storage is disabled by default.

You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in the App's footer, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

6. KaiSip mobile app

The KaiSip mobile app keeps your study data on your phone. In plain terms:

  • Your progress stays on your phone. Your answers, scores, how long you took to answer, how sure you said you were, when you first mastered each topic, saved questions, mistake journal, the notes you write on questions and settings are stored only on your device, unless you sign in and turn on sync (section 6A). Without an account they are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • App updates. The version of the app you install from Google Play gets its updates only through Google Play, like any other app. It does not contact Expo or any other update service. Test versions of the app (for example, builds shared with beta testers outside Google Play) work differently: each time a test version opens, it asks Expo's update service (EAS Update, run by Expo) whether a newer version of the app is available, and downloads it if so. That request carries the app's version, its update channel, your phone's platform (Android or iOS) and a random install code that Expo uses to roll updates out. Like any internet request, it also shows your IP address to Expo. It carries no study data, name, email address or account. Expo handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Your first name (optional). The app can ask for a first name so it can greet you. You can skip it, and change or remove it in Settings at any time. It is stored only on your phone (and in your own backup file, if you save one). It is never sent to us and never shown to other people.
  • Reminders are local. If you turn on study reminders, they are scheduled by your phone itself. No server is involved and no notification token is sent anywhere.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. An account is optional. The app collects no usage statistics, shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites. If you don’t create an account, nothing about your study leaves your phone.
  • Share cards are made on your phone. If you tap "Share progress", the app draws an image of the card you chose and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent to us; you decide where it goes. If you choose "Save Image", the app asks to add it to your Photos and never reads your photo library.
  • "Report a problem", "Suggest an idea" and "Contact support" use your own email app. Tapping one opens a draft in your email app with the item's code, the app version and your phone's system version filled in. Your theme, text size and Reduce Motion setting are added only if "Include display details" is ticked. Nothing is sent unless you send that email, and you can read and edit it first. If you send it, it reaches our support inbox (Gmail, run by Google) with your email address and message. We use them only to reply and to fix the problem, never for marketing. We keep support emails for up to 12 months after your issue is resolved, then delete them, or sooner if you ask. Please don't include questions from the real exam.
  • Backup files are made on your phone. If you tap "Save a backup" in Settings, the app puts your progress and settings, the notes you write on questions, and the list of problems you reported, into one file and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent to us. You decide where the file goes (for example Files, Google Drive or email), and that service keeps it under its own terms. Apart from anything you type into a note, the file contains no email address or device ID. The only name it can hold is the optional first name you entered in the app. "Restore from a backup" only reads the file you pick, and shows you what will change before anything is replaced.
  • Phone backups. If you have turned on your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup), that backup may include your KaiSip progress, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Apple or Google under their terms, not by us. If you are signed in to a KaiSip account, your sign-in is kept in your phone’s secure storage and is not part of that backup, so a restored phone starts signed out.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone. It does not remove backup files you saved elsewhere, or your KaiSip account if you have one; delete those where you keep them, or as section 6A describes. You can also reset your progress at any time in the app's Settings.

6A. Optional KaiSip account

You don’t need an account to use any study feature, in the app or on the website. An account lets you keep the same progress on your phone and on kaisip.com. You sign in with Google or with an 8-digit code we email you. KaiSip has no passwords.

What we keep

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send your codes.
  • An account ID.
  • Which sign-in methods you use (email code or Google), when the account was created and when you last signed in, and your active sign-in sessions (for each: when it started and was last used, and the IP address and the browser or app it came from), so you stay signed in and can sign out everywhere.
  • Which version of the terms you accepted, and that you confirmed you are 16 or older.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google tells our sign-in system your name and the link to your Google profile picture, and it stores them with your account. KaiSip doesn’t show or use them.

Sync

Sync starts only after you sign in, accept the terms and confirm you are 16 or older. The first time both your device and your account have progress, we show you what will change and you choose: combine them (the default, which loses nothing), use the account’s progress (your device’s is kept in an undo copy), or not now. In the app, sync is off until you turn it on (Settings → Account → Sync this phone).

While it is on, we keep one copy of your study progress and one of your study settings in your account: your answers (whether right, how sure you were and how long you took), reviews, your mistake journal, saved questions, the notes you write on questions, mocks, lessons and notes done, your study days and streak, badges, game levels, your readiness log and exam-week checklist, your plant, the questions you reported to us, your exam date and your study settings. The website takes part with the parts it has (answers, reviews, mistakes, saved questions, mocks, notes read, your notes on questions, study days and the exam date) and keeps everything else in the account as it was. The copy is stored compressed. With each copy we keep the app or website version that sent it, when it was sent, and a random code for the phone or browser that sent it (not a hardware or advertising ID; a new one is made before a different account syncs from the device, when you remove your progress from it, and when you delete your account).

We also keep one earlier daily copy of your progress, so support can help if something goes wrong. It is deleted when you delete the synced copy or your account.

These are never synced: study reminders, theme, motion and haptics settings, plant display choices, a quiz or mock in progress, your first name, the website’s cookie choice, and your sign-in session.

You can turn sync off at any time: that stops syncing, and the copy in your account stays until you delete it. You can delete the synced copy (Settings → Account → Delete synced progress in the app, or Your data → Account on the website: your account and the progress on your device stay), or delete your account. When you sign out you can also remove your progress from that device (for shared phones and computers); your account’s copy stays.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, keep your account safe, sync your progress and answer you. We do not sell it, show ads, track you, build profiles or use it to train AI models.

How we protect it

  • Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The database only lets each account read and change its own rows; the server checks the size and shape of everything it stores.
  • Sign-in codes work for 10 minutes, and deleting your account needs a fresh sign-in.
  • Backups are encrypted on a temporary GitHub build machine before they are stored, with a key only the maintainer holds. Sign-in sessions and one-time codes are never part of a backup.

Who processes it, and where

  • Supabase stores your account and synced progress in Singapore, under a data-processing agreement. Like any internet service, it keeps request logs and sign-in event logs (for example your IP address, your browser or app version, and when you signed in or asked for a code) for [Supabase’s log retention period on our plan], to keep the service secure. We don’t keep our own copy of sign-in events.
  • Resend (United States) sends your sign-in codes.
  • Cloudflare runs the human check on the website’s email form and keeps our encrypted backups (Cloudflare R2).
  • GitHub (United States) runs the nightly backup job, which encrypts the copy on a temporary build machine before storing it.
  • Google signs you in, if you choose Google.

How long we keep it

We keep it while your account exists. When you delete your account, we remove it from our live systems at once; our encrypted backups are kept for 30 days, so it is gone from them within 30 days. We keep only a record that an account was deleted: a one-way keyed code made from the account ID, the day (not the time) of the deletion, and how many feedback board posts and comments the account had (none during the beta). It holds no email address. We keep it for 3 years, so we can show the deletion happened, then delete it.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time, on one device or on every device.
  • Get a copy of your account data: Download my data (Settings → Account in the app, Your data → Account on the website). It includes your account details (with what Google shared and when you last signed in), your synced progress and settings with their device codes and times, and the daily support copy.
  • Delete everything: Delete account (Settings → Account in the app, Your data → Account on the website), the account deletion page, or email certprep.support@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. You will be asked to sign in again first, so we know it’s you.

Age

Accounts are for people aged 16 and over. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

Legal basis (EU/UK)

We run your account to provide the service you asked for (contract). Anything else happens only with your consent, which you can withdraw by deleting your account.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. These can include the right to access, correct, or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent.

Without an account, your progress is stored only on your own device, so you can exercise most of these rights yourself at any time by declining analytics, using "Cookie preferences", or clearing this site's data in your browser. In the mobile app, reset your progress in Settings or delete the app. With an account, use Download my data and Delete account (section 6A). For anything else — including requests relating to feedback messages — contact us using section 11. We answer within 30 days.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data for targeted advertising.

7A. Philippine Data Privacy Act

KaiSip is run from the Philippines, so the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) applies to the personal information in a KaiSip account, wherever you live.

  • Who is responsible: the personal information controller is [Developer name, exactly as shown on Google Play], the maintainer of KaiSip, [city], Philippines. Contact: certprep.support@gmail.com.
  • Data Protection Officer: [Data Protection Officer’s name], at the same address. Write "Privacy" in the subject.
  • Why we process it, and on what basis: to provide the account and sync you asked for, and with your consent, which you give when you create the account and can withdraw at any time by deleting it.
  • Who processes it for us, and where: Supabase (Singapore), Resend (United States), Cloudflare, GitHub (United States) and Google, as section 6A lists. Your data is therefore transferred outside the Philippines. We stay responsible for it wherever it is processed, and choose only providers bound to protect it.
  • How long: while your account exists, then as section 6A describes (gone from backups within 30 days; the deletion record kept 3 years).
  • Your rights as a data subject: to be informed, to access your data, to object, to have it corrected, erased or blocked, to data portability, to be indemnified for damages, and to file a complaint. In practice: Download my data (access and portability), Delete account (erasure, and withdrawing consent), and email us for anything else, including a correction.
  • Complaints: you can complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). We would be glad to hear from you first, but you don’t have to.
  • If something goes wrong: if a security breach puts your account data at real risk, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of knowing, where the law requires it.
