Privacy Policy

Draft prepared: 12 October 2026. Not in effect.

Aurivan is a free CISA exam-preparation app built privacy-first. You don’t need an account. Without one, your study progress never leaves your device. If you choose to create an account, we store only what is needed to sign you in and, if you turn on sync, to sync your progress. On the website we use anonymous analytics only if you accept it; the mobile app has no analytics at all. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Aurivan web application, the Aurivan mobile app for iPhone and Android, and their related pages ("the App"). Sections 2 to 5 describe the website; section 6 describes the mobile app, and section 6A the optional Aurivan account. Aurivan is operated as an independent project by its maintainer ("we", "us"). It is not a company offering, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ISACA.

2. Our privacy approach

The website runs entirely in your browser. It has no backend server and no database that we control. Because of this, the large majority of your activity — every question you answer, your scores, bookmarks, settings, and local profile — is stored only on your own device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

3. Information involved

3a. On-device data (never transmitted)

Your study progress, scores, bookmarks, review queue, exam-date setting, theme choice, and local profile name are saved in your browser’s localStorage. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data for this site removes it.

3b. Analytics (only with your consent)

The App uses Google Analytics, configured with Google Consent Mode. Before you make a choice, and if you choose "Decline", analytics storage is disabled — no analytics cookie and no analytics identifier are stored on your device.

Only if you select "Accept" on the cookie banner does Google Analytics record anonymous, aggregated usage (such as page views and which sections are visited) and set a cookie on your device to distinguish visits. This helps us understand how the App is used and where to improve it.

We do not collect names, email addresses, your answers, or your scores through analytics, and we do not use analytics for advertising.

3c. Feedback you choose to send

The App includes optional Feedback and Flag features. If you use one, the message you write is submitted through a Google Form to the maintainer’s email inbox. This happens only when you choose to send it. Please do not include sensitive personal information in these messages.

We keep these messages for up to 12 months after your issue is resolved, then delete them, or sooner if you ask.

3d. Hosting logs

The App is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the hosting provider may automatically process technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and protect against abuse. This is handled by the hosting provider under its own terms.

4. Cookies and consent

The App itself sets no cookies. The only cookie scenario is Google Analytics, described in section 3b, and it is used only after you accept on the cookie banner. Until you make a choice, analytics storage is disabled by default.

You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in the App’s footer, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

6. Aurivan mobile app

The Aurivan mobile app keeps your study data on your phone. In plain terms:

  • Your progress stays on your phone. Your answers, scores, how long you took to answer, how sure you said you were, when you first mastered each topic, saved questions, mistake journal and settings are stored only on your device, unless you turn on sync with an Aurivan account (section 6A).
  • App updates. Each time the app opens, it asks Expo’s update service (EAS Update, run by Expo) whether a newer version of the app is available, and downloads it if so. That request carries the app’s version, its update channel, your phone’s platform (Android or iOS) and a random install code that Expo uses to roll updates out. Like any internet request, it also shows your IP address to Expo. It carries no study data, name, email address or account. Expo handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Reminders are local. If you turn on study reminders, they are scheduled by your phone itself. No server is involved and no notification token is sent anywhere.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. An account is optional. The app collects no usage statistics, shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites. If you don’t create an account, nothing about your study leaves your phone.
  • Share cards are made on your phone. If you tap "Share progress", the app draws an image of the card you chose and hands it to your phone’s share sheet. Nothing is sent to us; you decide where it goes. If you choose "Save Image", the app asks to add it to your Photos and never reads your photo library.
  • "Report a problem", "Suggest an idea" and "Contact support" use your own email app. Tapping one opens a draft in your email app with the item’s code, the app version and your phone’s system version filled in. Your theme, text size and Reduce Motion setting are added only if "Include display details" is ticked. Nothing is sent unless you send that email, and you can read and edit it first. If you send it, it reaches our support inbox (Gmail, run by Google) with your email address and message. We use them only to reply and to fix the problem, never for marketing. We keep support emails for up to 12 months after your issue is resolved, then delete them, or sooner if you ask. Please don’t include questions from the real exam.
  • Backup files are made on your phone. If you tap "Save a backup" in Settings, the app puts your progress and settings, and the list of problems you reported, into one file and hands it to your phone’s share sheet. Nothing is sent to us. You decide where the file goes (for example Files, Google Drive or email), and that service keeps it under its own terms. The file contains no name, email address, account or device ID. "Restore from a backup" only reads the file you pick, and shows you what will change before anything is replaced.
  • Phone backups. If you have turned on your phone’s own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup), that backup may include your Aurivan progress, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Apple or Google under their terms, not by us. If you are signed in to an Aurivan account, your sign-in is kept in your phone’s secure storage and is not part of that backup, so a restored phone starts signed out.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone. It does not remove backup files you saved elsewhere, or your Aurivan account if you have one; delete those where you keep them, or as described in section 6A. You can also reset your progress at any time in the app’s Settings.

6A. Optional Aurivan account

You don’t need an account to use any study feature. If you choose to create one:

What we keep

  • Your email address, to sign you in.
  • An account ID.
  • Which sign-in methods you use (email code, Apple or Google), and when the account was created.
  • Which version of the terms you accepted, and that you confirmed you are 16 or older.

If you sign in with Apple, Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your email. We do not keep the name Apple shares.

Sync

Sync is off until you turn it on (Settings → Account → Sync this phone), and the app shows you what will change before it first syncs. While it is on, we keep one copy of your study progress and study settings in your account: your answers (whether right, how sure you were and how long you took), reviews, your mistake journal, saved questions, mocks, lessons and notes done, your study days and streak, badges, game levels, your readiness log and exam-week checklist, your plant, the questions you reported to us, your exam date and your study settings. With each copy we keep the app version that sent it, when it was sent, and a random install code for the phone that sent it (it is not a hardware or advertising ID; a new one is made before a different account syncs from that phone, and when you remove your progress from the phone or delete your account). We also keep the two most recent daily copies of your progress, so support can help if something goes wrong; they are deleted when you delete the synced copy or your account.

These stay on your phone and are never synced: study reminders, theme, motion and haptics settings, plant display choices, and a quiz in progress.

You can turn sync off at any time: that stops syncing, and the copy in your account stays until you delete it. You can delete the synced copy (Settings → Account → Delete synced progress: your account and the progress on your phone stay), or delete your account. Deleted copies are removed from our live systems at once and from backups within 30 days.

When you sign out, you can also remove your progress from that phone (for shared devices): your study progress, plant and study settings are removed from the phone, and your account’s copy stays.

Feedback board

If you use the feedback board with your account:

  • What others can see: the name you appear under (a pseudonym or a name you choose), your posts and comments, and when you made them. Who can read the board is set by us; the app tells you which applies (for example, only signed-in learners). Your email address is never shown.
  • What only you and our moderators see: your votes, who you follow and block, the reports you make, your appeals and your in-app notices.
  • What we keep to run and protect the board: the version of the community guidelines you agreed to and when; your standing (for example, a suspension and its end date); how many of your posts were approved; a count of your recent posts, votes and reports for rate limits (kept for 2 days); and a moderation record of every action we take (who acted, what and why, never the text of your post), kept for 12 months.
  • Filters: before a post appears we check it with word lists and by comparing it with our own practice questions; these checks run on our server and on your phone, and use no third-party or AI service. Some posts wait for a person to review them first.
  • Removed posts: we keep the text of a removed post or comment for 30 days so you can ask us to look again, then delete it. Real exam content is deleted at once.
  • Notices: replies, status changes and moderation notices appear in the app only (no push notifications, no emails).
  • You can withdraw a post or comment at any time. Deleting your account deletes your posts, comments, votes and blocks; reports you made about others stay with our moderators without your name.
  • “Download my data” includes your board name, posts, comments, votes, follows, blocks, reports, appeals and notices.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, keep your account safe and answer you. We do not sell it, show ads, track you, build profiles or use it to train AI models.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores the data in [region], under a data-processing agreement. Like any internet service, it keeps request logs (your IP address and your app's version, for example) for [Supabase's log retention period], to keep the service secure.
  • [Email provider] sends sign-in codes.
  • Apple or Google handles sign-in if you choose them.

Each must protect your data at least as well as this policy does.

How long we keep it

We keep it while your account exists. When you delete your account, we remove it from our live systems at once and from backups within 30 days. We keep only a record that an account was deleted, made with a one-way code from the account ID, with no email address, so we can show the deletion happened.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time, on this phone or on every device.
  • To get a copy of your account data, use Settings → Account → Download my data. It includes your synced progress and study settings; the two daily support copies are earlier versions of the same progress and are not included.
  • To delete everything, use Settings → Account → Delete account, visit [URL]/delete-account.html, or email certprep.support@gmail.com.

Age

Accounts are for people aged 16 and over.

Legal basis (EU/UK)

We run your account to provide the service you asked for (contract). Anything else happens only with your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. These can include the right to access, correct, or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent.

Without an account, your progress is stored only on your own device, so you can exercise most of these rights yourself at any time by declining analytics, using "Cookie preferences", or clearing this site’s data in your browser. In the mobile app, reset your progress in Settings or delete the app. With an account, use Download my data and Delete account in the app (section 6A). For anything else — including requests relating to feedback messages — contact us using section 11.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data for targeted advertising.

8. International visitors

Aurivan is used by candidates worldwide. The third-party services above may process data in countries other than yours. If you create an account, it is stored in [region] as described in section 6A.

9. Children’s privacy

Aurivan is a professional certification study tool intended for adults preparing for the CISA exam. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Accounts are only for people aged 16 and over.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves or as laws change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version. Material changes will be noted in the App.

11. Contact

For any privacy question or request, email certprep.support@gmail.com.
