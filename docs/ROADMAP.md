# Aurivan roadmap (mobile)

> **Internal: not shown in the app.** This list is for the team only. Until
> mobile 1.17.1 it was bundled in the app as the "What's next" screen; the
> owner removed that screen so the plan isn't a ready-made spec for anyone
> building a copy. Learners see changes only once they ship, in What's new
> (`WHATS_NEW` in `mobile/src/content/support/index.ts`).

Rules for this file:

- No dates and no promises: plans change as we learn what helps learners most.
- When an item ships, delete it here and describe the benefit (one plain
  sentence, no mechanics) in What's new.
- An idea from a learner joins this list only once the team agrees to plan it
  (`docs/support/PLAYBOOK.md`, step 5 of triage).
- Bigger product context lives in `docs/mobile/ARCHITECTURE.md` (roadmap and
  phases).

## In progress

### Lessons and games that come alive

Lesson steps that build up one at a time, and games with a look of their own.
Short fades instead of movement when Reduce Motion is on.

## Planned

### An optional account

Sign in only if you want to, and keep your progress on more than one device.
Everything keeps working without one. (Schema written in
`supabase/migrations/`, not yet applied.)

### A feedback board

Suggest ideas, see what other learners asked for, and read what we decide and
why.
