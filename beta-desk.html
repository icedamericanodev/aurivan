How the beta runs

The playbook.

Seven steps from signup to thank you. Nothing here is connected to Google Forms or Google Play, so every You step is done by hand.

Registration You

Import your Google Forms responses (Responses → Download CSV) or add a tester by hand. The same email is never added twice, whatever its capital letters or spaces.

Invitation You

Add their Google account to the closed test's tester list in Play Console and send the opt-in link. Then record the invite date.

Google Play opt-in You then Auto

When the tester has actually opted in and installed from Google Play, record that date. The 14-day review date is then worked out from the opt-in, never from the signup.

Feedback You

Record when feedback arrives and a short summary. The desk nudges you from day 10 if none has come in.

Participation check You

New testers qualify by 14 consecutive days in the Android test (feedback is encouraged, not required). Earlier sign-ups keep the terms they were offered, which for some also allow one piece of feedback. On or after the review date, check they stayed opted in every day. Choose one:

  • Continuous: opted in every day.
  • Technical gap, accepted: a break caused by a technical issue (describe it); you decide it still counts.
  • Interrupted: they left or paused for another reason, so they are not eligible.

Also confirm the eligibility terms they were offered, which are saved on their record.

Completion approval You

Approve when the tester's own terms are met. The database refuses an approval before day 14 (14-day route) or before the feedback date (feedback route, for earlier sign-ups whose terms include it).

CISA reward You then Auto

Switch on six months of CISA premium content and app study features for them yourself, then record the day you did it. The six months count from that activation day. Other certifications are not included.

What's automatic, and what isn't

Auto The desk works out

  • The review date: Google Play opt-in + 14 days
  • The reward end date: activation + 6 months
  • Each tester's stage and next step
  • Who needs attention, and follow-ups that are due
  • Duplicate emails, skipped on import and refused on save

You You do by hand

  • Download responses from Google Forms and import them
  • Send Play invites and confirm each opt-in in Play Console
  • Check continuous participation and technical issues
  • Approve completion
  • Switch on the CISA reward in the app, then record it

Looking after tester data

  • Only admins can read the roster. The database checks this on every request.
  • An exported CSV holds names and emails. Keep it private and delete it when you're done.
  • When a tester asks, or the programme ends, delete their record (open the tester → Delete).
  • Tell testers on your signup form that KaiSip keeps their answers to run the beta.
