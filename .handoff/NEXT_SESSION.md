## Quality bar (what "done" means for each domain)

## What "ISACA mindset" means in concrete terms (the 10 Golden Rules)

Every question's explanation should reinforce at least one:

1. Business risk over technical issues
2. Governance over operations
3. Prevention over correction
4. Detective over corrective (when prevention is not feasible)
5. Root cause over symptoms
6. Evidence over assumptions
7. Risk reduction over convenience
8. Independence and objectivity (auditor recommends, management decides)
9. Compensating controls when ideal controls are impossible
10. Business alignment in every IT decision

An explanation that just says "A is correct because it reduces risk" is OUT OF STANDARD. The fix is to teach: WHY the wrong answers tempt, WHY ISACA prefers the right one, WHICH principle is being tested, WHICH precision-word (FIRST / BEST / MOST / GREATEST / PRIMARY) changed the logic.
