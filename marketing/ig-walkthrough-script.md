# Aurivan Instagram walkthrough video script

A 60 to 90 second Taglish voiceover for an Instagram video that taps through
the Aurivan app on a phone. Generate the voiceover in ElevenLabs first, then
navigate the app to match. Upbeat and energetic. Estimated runtime about 75
to 85 seconds.

## Storyboard (navigation cue + Taglish voiceover)

1. Hook. Open the app, Home screen showing.
   "Mag-take ka ng CISA exam? Wag kang gagastos ng libo-libo. Eto, libreng reviewer. Aurivan."

2. Home tab. Slowly scroll the Home screen (readiness score, exam countdown, domain progress).
   "Pagbukas mo, nasa Home na agad ang importante: readiness score mo, countdown papuntang exam date, at progress mo per domain."

3. Tap Practice. Tap Start Practice, a question loads, tap an option, then tap a confidence button.
   "Punta tayo sa Practice. Totoong CISA-style na tanong. Sagutin mo, tapos i-rate mo kung gaano ka ka-confident: sure, educated guess, o guessing lang."

4. Tap Submit. Scroll through the explanation: why the answer is right, the option-by-option breakdown, the ISACA mindset note, and the tips.
   "Eto ang paborito ko. Hindi lang basta tama o mali. Ipapaliwanag kung bakit, bawat option, at kung paano talaga mag-isip si ISACA. May exam tips pa kada tanong."

5. Tap Mock. Show the Mock Exam setup card (150 questions, 4-hour timer).
   "May Mock Exam din. Buong 150 na tanong, four-hour timer, feel na feel mo ang totoong exam."

6. Tap Topics. Open a topic, scroll an illustrated study page (diagram, explanation, common mistakes).
   "Sa Topics, may kumpletong study guide. May diagrams, malinaw na paliwanag, at yung mga common mistakes na dapat mong iwasan."

7. Tap More then Weak Spots, then go back to Home and point at the Review queue.
   "Hindi mo alam kung saan ka mahina? Andiyan ang Weak Spots. At yung mga namali mo, babalik para ma-review mo ulit. Spaced repetition, ang galing."

8. Quick flashes. Open More and tap through Glossary, Principles, Saved; then tap the dark mode toggle.
   "May glossary, principles library, pwede mong i-save ang mahihirap na tanong, at oo, may dark mode."

9. Close. Back on Home or the logo.
   "Libre lahat to. Walang account, walang ads. Hindi to shortcut, pero sobrang laking tulong sa review mo. Aurivan. Nasa bio ang link. I-try mo na."

## Recording and sync tips

- ElevenLabs: use the Multilingual v2 model (it handles Taglish); pick an
  upbeat, younger-sounding voice. Generate the audio first.
- Then practice navigating the app to the audio two or three times before the
  real take, so each tap lands on its line.
- Beat 4 is the longest line; give yourself room to scroll the explanation
  unhurried while it plays.
- Add on-screen captions when editing. Most Instagram viewers watch on mute,
  and captions also reinforce the feature names.
- Keep the ethics rule: Aurivan is a big help but not a shortcut, never a
  guaranteed pass. Do not add pass-guarantee claims.
