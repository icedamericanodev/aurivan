## About the product
- **KaiSip** is an exam-prep app for IT audit, security and risk certifications, **starting with CISA®** (Certified Information Systems Auditor). It is on the web at **kaisip.com** and on **Android (Google Play, in beta)**, with **iPhone coming later**.
- **Tagline (use as the main line):** "Think like an IS auditor. CISA exam prep, taught the way the exam thinks."
- **Future certifications:** you may say "Starting with CISA. More certifications later." Never list future certifications or give dates.
- **Audience:**
  - IT auditors, accountants moving into IT audit, risk, compliance and security people preparing for CISA, worldwide.
  - They are busy working professionals who study in short sessions around their jobs.
  - Many fail practice questions because they think like a technician, not like an auditor.
- **What makes it different (use these, don't invent others):**
  1. **It teaches the "why".** Every answer explains the principle behind it, why the wrong options fall short, and three exam tips: *Eliminate, Final two, Exam cue*.
  2. **It builds the auditor's mindset.** For example: the auditor recommends and management decides. That is exactly what trips people up on the real exam.
  3. **Short, calm sessions:** a daily plan, 5-minute lessons, and brain games that train exam skills (spotting the trap answer, reading priority words like FIRST/BEST).
  4. **An honest, estimated readiness range,** not hype.
  5. **Full mock exams** weighted like the real exam.
  6. **A calm plant companion** grows as you make real progress (optional; a light touch, not the main message).
  7. **Privacy:** no account needed, the phone app works offline, and your progress stays on your device.
- **How the content is made (only if it comes up, e.g. in Reddit replies):** "Original questions and notes, written with AI assistance and checked against ISACA's published exam outline and standards." Never claim it was written by certified experts or comes from the real exam.
- **Brand personality:** calm, warm, smart, trustworthy. Think "a quiet forest study room", not "hustle". Never loud, never salesy.
- **Brand look:**
  - Deep forest green `#1E4A34` (lit top edge `#245A40`), warm paper `#F3F1EA`, ink `#17231B`, leaf green `#2B6E4A`, soft mint `#A9D9BA`. Dark mode background `#111613`.
  - **Fonts:** **Fraunces** (serif, for headlines and big moments) and **Figtree** (sans, for everything else). Both are free on Google Fonts.
  - **Logo:** the "Kasama" mark, a letter K made of three leaves, with the lowercase wordmark "kaisip".
- **Pronunciation of KaiSip:** "Kai-Sip" (two beats: KAI, then SIP). Write it as "Kai-Sip" in the voiceover script so text-to-speech says it right.

## The offer and call to action
- **Offer (decided):** "Beta testers get **6 months of KaiSip Premium free** once paid plans launch, and every tester is **thanked and credited** in the app (with their permission)."
  - KaiSip is **free to use during the beta**. Say exactly that. Never say "free app", "free forever" or "completely free", because paid plans are coming.
  - The 6 months **start on the day paid plans launch**, not on sign-up day. No card is needed, and nothing renews or charges.
  - Testers qualify by staying in the Android test for 14 days, or by sending at least one genuine piece of feedback. iPhone and website testers count too.
- **Why it's a win-win:** testers get a free tool for their own CISA review, and their feedback shapes the app.
- **Call to action:** "Join the beta" → the beta sign-up link I give you in my message . Android testers get an email with the Google Play test link when the test opens.
- **Platforms (decided):** the beta is for **Android phones (Google Play)**. iPhone users can study on the **website, kaisip.com**, today. The iPhone app is coming to the Apple App Store later; say "coming soon to iPhone", never a date.
- **What testers do:** install the test version, use it for 2 weeks, and send honest feedback.

## Rules you must follow (legal and platform)
- **Never** promise or imply a guaranteed pass, a pass rate, or "official" status.
- **Never** use ISACA's logo or imply endorsement. Add this end card / caption line: *"CISA® is a registered trademark of ISACA. KaiSip is not affiliated with or endorsed by ISACA."*
- **Do not** state how many questions the app has. Say "practice questions across all 5 CISA domains".
- **Never** ask for ratings or reviews in exchange for the reward. The reward is for **testing and feedback** only.
- **Reddit:** write like a real builder sharing their project ("I built this, looking for honest beta testers"), not like an ad. No hype words, no countdowns, no fake urgency.
- **Claims:** use only true claims from the list above.
- **Sound like a person, not AI:** simple everyday words, short sentences, first person ("I built…"). No em dashes. No buzzwords such as journey, unlock, elevate, game-changer, seamless, delve, revolutionize, empower. Keep my own voice from "My story" below.
- **Older drafts:** if I also attach older marketing drafts (e.g. a Reddit posting pack or an Instagram walkthrough script), use them for ideas only. Where they differ from this brief (for example, calling it a "free app"), **this brief wins**.

## What I need from you (deliverables)

   - Suggest a voice style: gender-neutral or my choice, age 30s, friendly professional, any accent that sounds natural to global English speakers.
4. **Captions:** burned-in subtitles text for every line (most people watch muted).
5. **Post text:**

   - a Reddit post title + body (and which subreddits fit; remind me to check each subreddit's self-promotion rules first)

   - 3–5 relevant hashtags where they fit (not on Reddit)
   - alt text for each video
6. **Build the video, if you have the tools:**
   - If you can use **ElevenLabs**, generate the voiceover. If you can use **Canva** or **Adobe Express**, assemble the video there.
   - If you can run code (for example Claude Code with ffmpeg or Remotion), render an MP4 from my screenshots: Ken Burns motion, brand fonts and colors, burned-in captions, voiceover track, and a soft royalty-free music bed you name (I'll supply it).
   - If you can't render, give me step-by-step instructions to build it in **CapCut** (free) using your scene table.
7. **Checklist before I post:** confirm every rule above is met.

Before you start, ask me anything you need: at most 5 questions, in one message. Then deliver everything in the order above.

## My story (why I built KaiSip)

**Full version (Reddit post, About page):**

I built KaiSip on the side, as a passion project.

When I was reviewing for the CISA exam while working full time, the hardest part wasn't the topics. It was finding the time. After a long day at work, I had piles of material and not much energy left. And the exam isn't cheap, so I really wanted to get it right the first time.

So I built the study tool I wished I had: short sessions that fit around work, and practice questions that explain why an answer is right, the way an auditor thinks, not just which letter to pick.

Honestly, I'm not a developer. AI tools made this possible. I spent time learning the basics of how software is built and followed a proper development process from start to finish, so the app would be useful and reliable.

Right now it's for CISA. If people find it helpful, I'd love to add more certifications later.

I'd really appreciate your feedback: what works, what doesn't, what's confusing. One honest note: since AI helped create the content, please still use your own judgment, and if something doesn't look right, tell me and I'll fix it.

**Short version (voiceover, about 20 seconds):**

I built Kai-Sip on the side. Reviewing for CISA while working full time was tough… too much material, not enough time. So I made the app I wished I had: short sessions, and questions that explain *why* the answer is right. It's in beta now, and I'd love your feedback.

**One-liner (Instagram Story / Threads):**

I'm a CISA reviewer who built the study app I wished I had. It's in beta, and I'm looking for testers.
