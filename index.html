Why Aurivan exists

Vision & Mission.

Our Vision

A world where every IT‑audit and risk professional can build genuine exam confidence — and a real auditor's judgment — regardless of budget, employer, or where they live.

Our Mission

To make rigorous CISA preparation free and honest — practice and study material that teaches how ISACA actually thinks, so candidates reason like an auditor instead of memorizing facts. A serious supplement to your study, never a shortcut around it.
