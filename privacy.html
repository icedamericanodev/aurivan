Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

KaiSip is a free CISA exam-preparation app built privacy-first. There are no accounts, and your study progress never leaves your device. On the website we use anonymous analytics only if you accept it; the mobile app has no analytics at all. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the KaiSip web application, the KaiSip mobile app for iPhone and Android, and their related pages ("the App"). Sections 2 to 5 describe the website; section 6 describes the mobile app. KaiSip is operated as an independent project by its maintainer ("we", "us"). It is not a company offering, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ISACA.

2. Our privacy approach

The website runs entirely in your browser. It has no backend server and no database that we control. Because of this, the large majority of your activity — every question you answer, your scores, bookmarks and settings — is stored only on your own device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

3. Information involved

3a. On-device data (never transmitted)

Your study progress, scores, bookmarks, review queue, exam-date setting and theme choice are saved in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data for this site removes it.

3b. Analytics (only with your consent)

The App uses Google Analytics, configured with Google Consent Mode. Before you make a choice, and if you choose "Decline", analytics storage is disabled — no analytics cookie and no analytics identifier are stored on your device.

Only if you select "Accept" on the cookie banner does Google Analytics record anonymous, aggregated usage (such as page views and which sections are visited) and set a cookie on your device to distinguish visits. This helps us understand how the App is used and where to improve it.

We do not collect names, email addresses, your answers, or your scores through analytics, and we do not use analytics for advertising.

3c. Feedback you choose to send

The App includes optional Feedback and Flag features. If you use one, the message you write is submitted through a Google Form to the maintainer's email inbox. This happens only when you choose to send it. Please do not include sensitive personal information in these messages.

We keep these messages for up to 12 months after your issue is resolved, then delete them, or sooner if you ask.

3d. Hosting logs

The website is hosted on Cloudflare. As with any website, the hosting provider may automatically process technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and protect against abuse. This is handled by the hosting provider under its own terms.

3e. Beta tester sign-up (optional)

If you sign up to test KaiSip before launch, you fill in a Google Form run by the maintainer, outside the App. It collects your email address, your phone type, your country, your exam timing (optional), how you found us, and the name or handle you would like credited (or none). We use it to add you to the Google Play test, to email you about the beta, to credit you if you asked, and to give you the tester reward.

It is stored in KaiSip's Google account (Google Forms and Sheets), and Android testers' email addresses are also added to Google Play Console's tester list. If you ask to be credited, the name you chose is shown to everyone in the mobile app's "Thank you, beta testers" screen; ask us at any time and it is removed in the next app update.

We keep sign-up data until your reward has been given and has ended, or until we confirm there will be no paid features, then delete it within 3 months. To leave or ask anything, email certprep.support@gmail.com.

4. Cookies and consent

The App itself sets no cookies. The only cookie scenario is Google Analytics, described in section 3b, and it is used only after you accept on the cookie banner. Until you make a choice, analytics storage is disabled by default.

You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in the App's footer, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

6. KaiSip mobile app

The KaiSip mobile app keeps your study data on your phone. In plain terms:

  • Your progress stays on your phone. Your answers, scores, how long you took to answer, how sure you said you were, when you first mastered each topic, saved questions, mistake journal, the notes you write on questions and settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • App updates. Each time the app opens, it asks Expo's update service (EAS Update, run by Expo) whether a newer version of the app is available, and downloads it if so. That request carries the app's version, its update channel, your phone's platform (Android or iOS) and a random install code that Expo uses to roll updates out. Like any internet request, it also shows your IP address to Expo. It carries no study data, name, email address or account. Expo handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Your first name (optional). The app can ask for a first name so it can greet you. You can skip it, and change or remove it in Settings at any time. It is stored only on your phone (and in your own backup file, if you save one). It is never sent to us and never shown to other people.
  • Reminders are local. If you turn on study reminders, they are scheduled by your phone itself. No server is involved and no notification token is sent anywhere.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app has no sign-in, collects no usage statistics, shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Share cards are made on your phone. If you tap "Share progress", the app draws an image of the card you chose and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent to us; you decide where it goes. If you choose "Save Image", the app asks to add it to your Photos and never reads your photo library.
  • "Report a problem", "Suggest an idea" and "Contact support" use your own email app. Tapping one opens a draft in your email app with the item's code, the app version and your phone's system version filled in. Your theme, text size and Reduce Motion setting are added only if "Include display details" is ticked. Nothing is sent unless you send that email, and you can read and edit it first. If you send it, it reaches our support inbox (Gmail, run by Google) with your email address and message. We use them only to reply and to fix the problem, never for marketing. We keep support emails for up to 12 months after your issue is resolved, then delete them, or sooner if you ask. Please don't include questions from the real exam.
  • Backup files are made on your phone. If you tap "Save a backup" in Settings, the app puts your progress and settings, the notes you write on questions, and the list of problems you reported, into one file and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent to us. You decide where the file goes (for example Files, Google Drive or email), and that service keeps it under its own terms. Apart from anything you type into a note, the file contains no email address or device ID. The only name it can hold is the optional first name you entered in the app. "Restore from a backup" only reads the file you pick, and shows you what will change before anything is replaced.
  • Phone backups. If you have turned on your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup), that backup may include your KaiSip progress, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Apple or Google under their terms, not by us.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone. It does not remove backup files you saved elsewhere; delete those where you keep them. You can also reset your progress at any time in the app's Settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. These can include the right to access, correct, or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent.

Because the App holds no accounts and stores your progress only on your own device, you can exercise most of these rights yourself at any time by declining analytics, using "Cookie preferences", or clearing this site's data in your browser. In the mobile app, reset your progress in Settings or delete the app. For anything else — including requests relating to feedback messages — contact us using section 11.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data for targeted advertising.

8. International visitors

KaiSip is used by candidates worldwide. The third-party services above may process data in countries other than yours. By using the App with analytics accepted, you understand that anonymous usage data may be processed by Google in that manner.

9. Children's privacy

KaiSip is a professional certification study tool intended for adults preparing for the CISA exam. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves or as laws change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version. Material changes will be noted in the App.

11. Contact

For any privacy question or request, email certprep.support@gmail.com.
