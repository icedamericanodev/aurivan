Help and contact

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Aurivan is a free, independent exam-preparation app made by a small team. We read every message. Write to us at:

certprep.support@gmail.com

1. Where to get help

In the mobile app

  • Settings → Help and feedback (or You → Get help or send feedback) has common questions, Contact support, Report a problem and Suggest an idea.
  • A problem in a question, study note or lesson step? Tap Report a problem right under it. In a game, use the link in the round's recap. Your email app opens with the details filled in, including the item's code, so we know exactly which one. You can read and change everything before you send it.
  • Mock exams have no report button while the clock runs. Report a question from the review after you submit.

On the website

  • Use Feedback at the top of the page (or in the menu on a phone), or the Flag button on a question.
  • Common questions are under More → FAQ & Help.

2. What to include

A few details help us fix things faster:

  • For a question: its code (for example d4_217; in the mobile app it's shown under the answer; on the website the Flag form adds it for you), what looks wrong, and which answer you think is best and why.
  • For an app problem: what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. In the mobile app, the version is in Settings → About; your phone's system version helps too.
  • Screenshots are welcome if they show the problem.

You never need to send a password, your exam date, your scores or a backup file. If a backup file would really help with a data problem, we'll ask first and explain what it contains.

Please don't send or post questions from the real exam. Candidates agree to keep exam content confidential, so we can't accept or use it, and we delete it if it arrives. Every Aurivan question is written from scratch. Tell us the topic you'd like more practice on instead.

3. Common questions

How do I move my progress to a new phone? (mobile app)

In the app, go to Settings → Your data and tap Save a backup. Keep the file somewhere the new phone can reach, such as Files, Google Drive or your email. On the new phone, choose Restore from a backup on the welcome screen, or later in Settings. Aurivan never uploads your progress.

Why is my readiness a range?

Readiness is an estimate from your answers, weighted by how much each domain counts on the exam. With fewer answers it is less certain, so it shows as a range that narrows as you answer more. It is a study estimate, not a prediction of your exam result.

Why did my review count change?

Questions come back for review on a schedule. Answer one right and it comes back later; miss it and it comes back sooner. Lucky guesses come back too, so the number due goes up and down from day to day.

Why don't I get study reminders? (mobile app)

Reminders stay off until you turn them on in Settings, under Study, and your phone must allow notifications for Aurivan. You get at most one a day, on the days you choose.

What happens after I report a problem?

A person reads it and checks it. When a report leads to a fix, it is listed in What's new in the app, and the app on your phone marks your report as updated. Often the item turns out to be right as it is; when that happens we reply and explain why.

4. Your privacy

In the mobile app, nothing is sent by itself: you send these emails from your own email account. When you do, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply and to fix the problem.

On the website, Feedback and Flag send your message through a Google Form to the same inbox. We don't get your email address unless you type it in.

We keep support messages for up to 12 months after your issue is resolved, then delete them, or sooner if you ask. See the Privacy Policy for details.
